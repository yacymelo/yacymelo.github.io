I am an Associate Professor in the School of Software Engineering at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST). My research focuses on processing-in-memory architectures and hardware–software co-design for LLM inference, agentic serving, and operator optimization.

I earned my bachelor’s degree in Computer Science and Technology from HUST in 2016 and my Ph.D. there in 2022 under the supervision of Prof. Xiaofei Liao. From 2022 to 2024, I conducted postdoctoral research at HUST and Zhejiang Lab under the mentorship of Prof. Hai Jin.

Research Areas

  • Processing-in-memory & near-data processing — Design of in- and near-memory computing architectures and implementation of systems on real processing-in-memory hardware.
  • Parallel computing systems — Parallel systems for LLM inference and agentic serving, and automated optimization of operator kernels using agents with recursive self-improvement.

Join our group

Our group studies processing-in-memory architectures and systems for LLM inference. I welcome inquiries about the Ph.D. position, future graduate openings, and undergraduate research opportunities.
